A password manager that keeps your secrets on your device.

By Erick — one developer, no accounts, no data harvesting.

Hi, I'm Erick. UCrypt is a browser extension that stores your passwords in a vault encrypted with your own key. The vault lives in your browser, on your machine. There is no account to create and no server of mine holding a copy of your stuff.

If you want a backup, UCrypt can save an encrypted copy of your vault to your own Google Drive — not my Drive, yours. The file is encrypted before it ever leaves your device, so all Google ever holds is a blob that nobody can read without your key. Not me. Not Google. Nobody.

What it does

  • A vault that never leaves your device, encrypted with your key
  • A password and passphrase generator, with wordlists built in
  • Optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive
  • Sync between your own devices, with recovery words as the way back in
  • Export and import, so you are never locked in

Get it

UCrypt is on the Chrome Web Store.

Install UCrypt from the Chrome Web Store

Works in Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers.

Why I built it

Most password managers want your email, your subscription, and your trust in their servers. I wanted the opposite: a tool that works offline, keeps everything local, and only talks to the cloud when you tell it to — and even then, only to a cloud folder that belongs to you, not to me.

If that sounds like your kind of tool, give it a try. And whatever you do, write down your recovery words and keep them safe. That is the one thing I can't fix for you later.

    Incoming files

    Checking for incoming files every 60 s…

    Nothing is received without your explicit Accept. Received files are never opened automatically.

    The flow — pick, send, verified, received
    1. Pick a file and the device to send it to. Your devices are your chain — Cloud Sync adds them.
    2. UCrypt connects directly first. If the direct road cannot reach, the file continues through your cloud on its own — the line tells you which path is in use.
    3. Delivery is proven: the line shows the file's hash, and the other device verifies it before saving.
    4. Incoming files appear in "Incoming files" — nothing is saved without your Accept.
    No cloud, no screen? Use a file

    Export the file as one offer file, move it however you like (USB, email), and import it on the other device. Works fully offline.
